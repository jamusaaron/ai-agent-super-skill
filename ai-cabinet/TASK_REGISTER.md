## TASK-001 — recommended first test task

A deliberately non-sensitive shakedown that exercises every active role and the
full Case Room handoff without touching a single restricted action or external
system:

> **Produce a one-page "Cabinet Operations Brief".**
> Chief writes the brief; Counsel checks the Standing Orders wording for gaps
> or ambiguity; Ledger tallies the registers (roles active vs proposed, files
> created, tasks open); Quill drafts the one-pager; Sceptic verifies every
> claim in it against the actual files; Folio files it and updates this
> register; Chief returns it to Jamie.

Runs entirely inside this workspace. Nothing is sent, published, submitted,
deleted, overwritten, purchased, re-permissioned, or fetched from any
departmental system.
